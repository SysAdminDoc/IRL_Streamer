## Final validation disposition

All 145 states have complete current, result, overlay, diff, and side-by-side artifacts. The configured SSIM threshold remained 0.985; no threshold was lowered and no region was masked or waived. The final result is 0 strict passes and 145 retained failures, with median SSIM 0.836694 and maximum 0.930146. These accepted deviations explain legitimate differences but do not convert a failed metric into a pass.

Production streaming/capture behavior remains blocked by D005 and D012. Pixel-equivalent reconstruction remains blocked by D001–D004, D006–D009, D011, and remaining app-owned raster differences recorded in `design-qa.md`.
